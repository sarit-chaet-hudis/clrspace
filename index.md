## intent for project
=================
Clrspace is a transitional headspace between a scatterbrain intuitive state and creational resourceful thinking and executing state.
It's aimed at appealing to me when I want to start consuming content that's both visual and intellectually slightly challenging, like a minigame or an easy puzzle or read something short.
It contains also several types of means of self expression, be it a canvas, a color picker, a place to write some thoughts, a start of something that I can easily pick up.
It looks very diverse visually in terms of colors, styles, interaction and ui design, but overall has a tendency towards pixel art, colorful schema and colored transitions that yield a harmonious joyful feeling.
It has some interactions in it that change the overall look and feel of clrspace like the background music, resolution, quality of transitions, heat or coolness of colors and more.
It has a feeling of infinity and as I scroll into different directions I discover more. Can I go back to a place I once visited? I'm not sure.
It only needs 1 or two key capabilities that connect the user to the world outside clrspace - a text keeper/ exporter and a visual keeper/ exporter. Maybe for capturing video as well.
